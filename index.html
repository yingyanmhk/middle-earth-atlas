Contents

Six books, and the road of the Ring through them. Each place is a miniature standing in the Red Book of Westmarch; click a place to travel there, click a book to read its interlude.

Each diorama is generated procedurally in the browser from a few hundred lines of code. The texts are paraphrases written for this atlas; read the book itself.
